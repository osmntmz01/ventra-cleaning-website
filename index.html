FAQ

Common questions.

How does ozone treatment actually work?▼
Ozone (O₃) circulates through the treated space and reacts with odour-causing compounds in air, fabric, carpet and vents. It attacks the source instead of covering it with fragrance, then converts back to oxygen after treatment.
Is ozone treatment safe?▼
Yes, when it is handled professionally and the space is empty during treatment. No people, pets or plants remain inside while ozone is running. We ventilate the area before re-entry and provide safety instructions before each job.
How long does treatment take?▼
Vehicle ozone is booked for about 2 hours. Seat cleaning and spot cleaning take about 1 hour. Seat and floor cleaning takes about 2 hours. Whole house ozone is booked at 1 hour per 36 cubic metre room.
Can you treat cars and used vehicles?▼
Yes. We treat smoke, pet, food, milk, vomit and stale interior odours trapped in seats, carpet, headliners and air vents. It is ideal for used cars, rideshare vehicles and cars bought with a smell already inside.
Can you treat homes, rooms and rentals?▼
Yes. We treat rooms, homes, rentals, carpets, sofas, rugs and upholstery across Melbourne. Ozone is especially useful for stale indoor smells, damp areas, pet odours and smoke left behind by previous occupants.
What odours can you treat?▼
We treat smoke odours, pet and urine odours, dog smells, damp and musty smells, fish and cooking odours, milk, vomit, food spills, car interior odours, upholstery odours and carpet odours. Stubborn smells are exactly what we are built for.
What do I need to do before treatment?▼
Remove people, pets, plants and any sensitive items from the treatment area. For best results, clear loose rubbish, wet items and personal belongings so ozone and spot cleaning can reach the affected surfaces properly.
When can I re-enter the space?▼
After ozone treatment, we ventilate the room, home or vehicle before re-entry. Re-entry is usually within 30 to 60 minutes after ventilation, depending on the space and job size. We will confirm when it is ready.
